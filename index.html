About

I'm a computer science student at Northeastern University. Most of my time goes toward writing code, learning how systems work under the hood, and building software that has to deal with real-world messiness schedules that change, trucks that show up late, warehouses that run on spreadsheets and phone calls.

I'm a co-founder of Movomint, a logistics software platform for managing warehouse operations. We built it because the tools that existed for appointment scheduling, dock management, and driver check-ins were either outdated or nonexistent. A lot of facilities were still tracking everything manually, and we wanted to fix that. The product keeps evolving as we learn more from the people actually using it.

Outside of school and work, I run. I'm training for a marathon right now. It's a good counterweight to sitting at a computer all day, and the structure of a training plan building up mileage week over week, managing recovery, staying consistent when it's not fun carries over to how I try to approach everything else.

On the technical side, I'm interested in systems programming, infrastructure, and the kind of software that sits close to real operations. I like understanding how things work a layer or two below where most people look.
